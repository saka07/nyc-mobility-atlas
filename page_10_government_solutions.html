C01: Eastern Queens

Fresh Meadows to Midtown Manhattan

Proposed for Testing
Measured Difficulty: Walking access and transfers consume 17.7 minutes (31.8% of the trip). The bus ride is only 6.9 minutes.
Proposed Response: Feeder bus lane priority and Transit Signal Priority on Union Tpke/Utopia Pkwy, plus sheltered transfer bays at 169th St / Kew Gardens.
Why It Helps: Reduces feeder delay and protects transfer connections to express subway. First-mile pedestrian burden remains a constraint.
Missing Evidence: Neighbourhood-specific peak car trips entering CBD; exact Q30 headway regularity.
C02: East Bronx

Throggs Neck to Midtown Manhattan

Proposed for Testing
Measured Difficulty: Total trip exceeds 71 minutes; feeder bus leg is 16.0 minutes in mixed traffic, followed by 42.5-minute #6 subway.
Proposed Response: Feeder queue bypass lanes and TSP along Westchester Ave / Crosby Ave connecting to #6 train at Buhre Ave.
Why It Helps: Reduces feeder bus delay and headway variability connecting to rapid transit.
Missing Evidence: Specific Bx8 bottleneck locations; local vehicle ownership rates in Throggs Neck.
C03: Southeast Queens

Rosedale to Lower Manhattan (Nostrand Ave)

Proposed for Testing
Measured Difficulty: Rapid LIRR rail run (27 min), but transferring to subway requires an 8.5-minute outdoor walk with double fares.
Proposed Response: Enclosed intermodal transfer concourse between LIRR Nostrand Ave and A/C subway stations; full OMNY cross-honoring.
Why It Helps: Eliminates physical transfer friction and financial double-payment, unlocking fast commuter rail capacity.
Missing Evidence: Capital engineering feasibility; operating revenue impact of fare integration.
C04: Eastern Queens Feeder

Union Turnpike Feeder Bus Priority & Kew Gardens Hub

Proposed for Testing
Measured Difficulty: Feeder bus run times along arterial reach 21 to 41 minutes; transfer friction at Kew Gardens interchange adds 8 minutes.
Proposed Response: Peak bus lanes, Transit Signal Priority (TSP), off-board OMNY payment, and sheltered intermodal transfer bays.
Why It Helps: Tackles both feeder in-vehicle delay and transfer dwell, connecting passengers to fast E/F subway trunk.
Missing Evidence: Tract-level driver destination shares into tolled core; on-street TSP conflict points with crosstown traffic.
