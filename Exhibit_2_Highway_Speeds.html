Highway speeds barely changed overall between 2024 and 2025

80 highway links with good data, weekday mornings 7–10 a.m., March to June. Hover over or tap a road for details.

What this shows

  • 13 links got faster, 45 barely changed and 22 got slower (by more than 2 mph).
  • The median change was −0.37 mph (−1.0%): essentially flat.
  • Borough medians range from −6.0 mph in Brooklyn to +0.4 mph in Manhattan.

Read with care

  • This compares two periods. It does not show that tolling caused any change.
  • Brooklyn has only 4 links. One BQE stretch fell 20.7 mph; deck repairs were under way at the time, but this data cannot show the cause.
  • Only links with at least 65 good weekdays (of 86) in both years are included.

Source: NYC DOT traffic speed data (Socrata i4gi-tjb9), 1 March – 30 June 2024 and 2025.
