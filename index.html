What we recommend

Start on Union Turnpike in Eastern Queens: faster buses and an easier transfer at Kew Gardens. In a model test, trips from six Union Turnpike origins to Midtown East at 8 a.m. are 6.2–12.5 minutes shorter. Measure the Q30 and Bx8 buses before doing more.
