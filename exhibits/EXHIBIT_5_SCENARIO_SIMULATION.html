Key Government Takeaways:
1. Leveraged Impact: Surface bus priority (S1: -23% bus run time) combined with intermodal station transfer upgrades (S2: -3 min dwell/walk) yields 6.2 to 12.6 minutes in net door-to-door savings (10.3% to 16.5% overall journey improvement).
2. Inherent Distance Reality: Even with aggressive capital investments, outer-borough commutes remain 46 to 70 minutes; interventions make transit competitive and reliable, not instantaneous.
