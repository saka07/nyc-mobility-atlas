Exhibit 5 · Union Turnpike what-if test

A faster bus saves the most time on Union Turnpike: 6 to 12.5 minutes a trip, against about 4 in Throggs Neck

Six starting points along Union Turnpike to Midtown East (Grand Central), leaving at 8 a.m. The test assumes the Union Turnpike bus runs 30% faster and the change to the subway takes 3 minutes less. Other destinations and times give different results.

Door-to-door trip time (minutes)

Where the time saved comes from (minutes)

What this shows

  • Savings range from 6.2 min (Hillcrest) to 12.5 min (Glen Oaks): 12–15% of each trip, 8.7 min on average.
  • Faster buses save 3.2–9.5 min (more for longer bus rides); the easier transfer saves 3.0 min on every trip.
  • The same test in Throggs Neck saves about 3.7 minutes (5%), because the bus is only 16 of the 71 minutes there.

What this does not show

  • How many drivers would switch. This is a what-if estimate, not a forecast of riders.
  • The walk to the bus in Fresh Meadows. Only the Union Turnpike bus is tested.
  • Trips still take 46 to 70 minutes after the change.

Source: what-if calculation on trip-planner estimates (OpenTripPlanner, MTA fall 2026 timetables). Throggs Neck uses the bus speed-up only, because its change to the train is a 3-minute walk.
