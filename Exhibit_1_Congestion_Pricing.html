Exhibit 1 · Congestion pricing results

Congestion pricing cut traffic into the zone by about 11%

About 67,000 fewer vehicles a day entered Manhattan south of 60th Street in the first six months (5 Jan – 30 Jun 2025).

Daily vehicle entries, before and after tolling (official estimate)

Average weekday entries by crossing, 2025 (all 12 crossings)

What this shows

  • Entries fell by about 67,000 a day (−11%) compared with before tolling.
  • The busiest crossing is East 60th St (15.5% of entries), followed by the FDR Drive (10.4%).
  • On the map, the shaded area is the official zone; circle size shows weekday entries at each crossing.

Read with care

  • The before and after totals are rounded official estimates (about 600,000 and 533,000).
  • Crossing counts (511,829 a weekday in total) use a different detection method, so they do not add up to the official totals.
  • This shows what changed, not why every change happened.

Sources: MTA congestion pricing six-month report; NY Open Data t6yz-b64h (5 Jan – 30 Jun 2025); zone boundary from the MTA GIS layer.
